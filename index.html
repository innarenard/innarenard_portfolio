About me

I own the end-to-end product design process – from UX research I run myself and hypothesis testing to design review and post-launch user surveys. My decisions are data-driven and balance business goals, user needs and technical constraints. I define success metrics before I start designing.

I work in cross-functional teams with product managers and engineers. I explain and defend my design decisions to stakeholders using research and competitor analysis.

I set up AI for each task: I write rules, acceptance criteria and custom Claude skills, so the results are predictable. That’s how I built Lunel: Claude Code wrote the code, and I reviewed and approved the result.

What I love most is the detective work: finding the problem, getting to the root cause and solving it. In B2B, I also get to learn someone else’s profession. In B2C, the challenge is to attract users and keep them.
