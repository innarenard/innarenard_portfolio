About

What keeps me in B2B is that it makes you learn someone else's profession. The result is not only metrics and revenue: people who have no choice but to use a tool start using it willingly and faster. You get to change both the business and how someone's working day goes.

B2C is interesting for the opposite reasons – bolder ideas, a different approach, the job of attracting people and keeping them.

There are two parts of the work I love most. The detective part: find the problem, find the solution, and reconcile what the business wants, what people need and what the technology allows. And building the tool itself – something good-looking and comfortable that a person uses to get their own job done.

And I love teams that trade knowledge and experience: when there are other designers around who care, you grow faster.
