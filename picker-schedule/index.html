Business problem

Administrators scheduled staff without accounting for peak loads, leading to a shortage of pickers during evening hours and causing delays
Too many people are responsible for pickers’ dark store shifts — administrators and coordination staff alike

The main insight

Research revealed that the method of scheduling pickers’ shifts differs from dark store to dark store

Pickers indicate their availability through a courier app, causing errors when a staff member is still working as a courier

Pickers write in chats and the admin personally contacts coordination staff when a picker needs to be brought in

Task

Based on the research results presented to the business, a decision was made to create a scheduling section for pickers.

A section needs to be created where pickers can specify their available shift time. Coordination staff will be responsible for confirming the shift at the dark store.

Hypotheses

A dedicated section in the picker app will prevent errors caused by multi-role assignments

Removing the administrator from the process will reduce fraud
